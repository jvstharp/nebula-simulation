# Nebula Corporate World: Structure and Approach

## 1) World model
Nebula uses a **hybrid corporate world**: a stable base layer with controlled evolution.

### Stable layer
These elements stay persistent across sessions:
- Company identities, sectors, and cultures
- Org charts and reporting lines
- Core employee personas and senior leadership
- Role definitions and competency expectations
- Each company’s strategic positioning and operating style

### Dynamic layer
These elements change over time:
- Case studies and business problems
- Market conditions and competitive pressure
- Internal politics, stakeholder behavior, and urgency
- Budget changes, hiring needs, product setbacks, and deadlines
- Relationships between employees, teams, and companies

## 2) Why this hybrid approach
This structure keeps the world believable and secure while still making every simulation feel fresh.

- Stability gives users continuity and immersion.
- Controlled evolution prevents repetition.
- Dynamic scenarios keep the experience varied.
- Persistent memory makes actions matter over time.

## 3) Simulation rules
- No case study is hard-coded.
- Scenario generation must adapt to user seniority and role.
- Character behavior must remain consistent with each persona profile.
- AI-generated content must stay inside the world logic.
- User actions should influence future company state, relationships, and scenario difficulty.

## 4) World scope
Initial scope:
- One corporate universe
- Multiple companies across selected industries
- Three core domains: Marketing, Data/Analysis, Consulting
- Reporting hierarchies for every company
- Persistent employee and company memory

## 5) Core design principles
- Stable identity, dynamic consequence
- Realistic corporate behavior
- Role-based progression
- Evidence-based feedback
- Secure isolation between company worlds when needed
- No direct answer leakage from AI systems
