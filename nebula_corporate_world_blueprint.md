# Nebula Corporate World: Industry, Domain, Company, and Org Blueprint

## 1) Target graduate industries
Recommended top 5 industries for early-career users:
1. Technology
2. Consulting
3. Financial Services
4. Consumer Goods / Retail
5. Healthcare / Life Sciences

These industries are common graduate destinations, provide strong career progression, and map well to structured simulation scenarios.

---

## 2) Core domains inside the platform
Nebula initially focuses on three core domains that appear across all industries:
- Marketing
- Data / Analysis
- Consulting

Each domain should have 3–5 roles available in the simulation.

### Marketing roles

- Brand Manager

## 4) Common company hierarchy pattern
Every company should include:
- Executive layer: CEO and one to three C-level or equivalent leaders
- Functional leads: product, marketing, data, operations, finance, HR, legal, or client-facing leads as relevant
- Mid-level managers: managers or leads who report upward
- Individual contributors: specialists, analysts, consultants, or product managers

Reporting lines must be explicit so the simulation can model authority, approvals, escalation, and conflict.

---

## 5) Minimum employee profile structure
Each employee should have a detailed profile with:
- Name
- Role
- Department
- Seniority level
- Reporting manager
- Personality traits
- Communication style
- Goals
- Frustrations
- Risk tolerance
- Performance strengths
- Performance gaps
- Relationship scores with key stakeholders

---

## 6) Simulation environment rules
- Companies must persist across sessions.
- Personas must retain memory of prior interactions.
- Case studies must evolve even when the company stays the same.
- New scenarios should reuse the same corporate world while changing the challenge, context, and pressure.
- Cross-company relationships should exist where relevant, such as partnerships, competition, recruiting, vendor relationships, or alumni links.
- All content must be generated dynamically inside the world logic, not hard-coded as fixed conversations.

---

## 7) Recommended additional world data
To make the environment feel real, each company should also have:
- Current business priorities
- Market position
- Internal risk level
- Budget status
- Team morale
- Hiring needs
- Active projects
- External competitors
- Relationship map to other companies

This data gives the simulation memory, tension, and continuity.
